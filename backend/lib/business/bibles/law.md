## HOW A LAW FIRM ACTUALLY OPERATES

### The Core Business Model
- Law firms operate on **billable hour × realization × collection** — three numbers that compound or destroy the firm
- **Utilization Rate** = Billable Hours Worked / Available Hours. 65–75% healthy for associates, 50–65% for partners
- **Realization Rate** = Billed $ / Worked $. Above 90% healthy. Below 80% = pricing / scoping problem
- **Collection Rate** = Collected $ / Billed $. Above 95% healthy. Below 85% = AR discipline collapse
- **Effective Hourly Rate** = Collected $ / Worked Hours. The only number that actually matters.

### Practice Area Variants (cash dynamics differ wildly)
1. **Family Law** — retainer-based, often replenished, emotional clients, AR risk
2. **Personal Injury** — contingency, zero cash until settlement, file load capital-intensive
3. **Real Estate (closings, transactional)** — flat-fee, fast-cycle, volume game, automation-friendly
4. **Wills & Estates** — flat-fee + hourly hybrid, referral-driven
5. **Corporate / Commercial** — hourly + flat-fee hybrid, larger matters, longer cycles
6. **Immigration** — flat-fee, government delay risk, AR mostly upfront
7. **Criminal Defence** — flat-fee or retainer, collection upfront critical
8. **Employment Law** — hourly + contingency hybrid, mid-cycle matters
9. **Civil Litigation** — hourly, multi-year matters, WIP-heavy
10. **IP / Tech / Startup** — hourly + flat-fee + equity hybrid, sophisticated clients

Always confirm the practice mix in the first session and tailor every output to it.

### The Trust Account: Where Careers End
- Every dollar of client money sits in a **trust account** (IOLTA in US, mixed trust in CA per province)
- **Three-way reconciliation daily**: bank balance = trust ledger total = sum of client sub-ledgers
- Variance not resolved same day = Law Society / state bar reporting territory
- Negative balance on ANY client sub-ledger = immediate regulator notification
- One mistake here can disbar a lawyer permanently. This is the #1 thing Jarvis protects against.

### The AR Engine: Where Firms Bleed
- Lawyers are notoriously bad at chasing money — feels unprofessional, breaks rapport
- **AR Aging**: <10% should sit over 90 days. Above 15% = AR discipline collapse
- **WIP Aging**: anything unbilled >60 days should be billed or written off
- **Avg Days to First Bill**: matter open → first invoice. <30 days is healthy. >45 days = systemic failure
- The 3-tier collection script (soft → firm → final) sent on schedule recovers 60–80% of stale AR

### The Intake Engine: Where Firms Grow or Die
- **Intake-to-Retained conversion**: 25–40% healthy. Below 20% = leak
- Most firms answer 40–60% of intake calls. Voicemail is where new matters die.
- 24/7 AI voice intake + same-call conflict check + same-day engagement letter = 2–3x conversion lift
- Source attribution: every new matter logged with referral source, no exceptions

## THE 25 PROBLEMS THAT KILL LAW FIRMS

1. **Trust account not reconciled daily** — career-ending exposure ignored
2. **AR >90 days >15% of total AR** — write-offs hidden in optimism
3. **Realization <80%** — partners refuse to scope or bill what they worked
4. **Collection rate <85%** — invoices sent then never followed up
5. **Avg days to first bill >45** — matter opens, work happens, nobody invoices
6. **WIP >60 days unbilled** — cash sitting in associates' timesheets
7. **Intake calls answered <60% of the time** — new matters die in voicemail
8. **Intake-to-Retained <20%** — no script, no conflict check, no same-day engagement letter
9. **Conflict checks not logged** for opened matters — regulator territory
10. **Limitation period within 30 days** on an open matter with no filing prep
11. **Court deadline within 48h** with no filing prepared
12. **One referral source >50% of new matters** in last 90 days — concentration risk
13. **Lawyer utilization <40%** trailing 30 days — paying for nothing
14. **Associate billing dropped >20% MoM** with no PTO explanation
15. **Negative Google review about fees or communication** unanswered >24h
16. **Retainer balance below threshold** on active matter — working without security
17. **No matter profitability by practice area** — partners can't see which work is worth doing
18. **Marketing budget without source attribution** — burning cash on channels that don't bring matters
19. **Practice management software not integrated** with accounting — double entry, errors compound
20. **Engagement letter not signed** before work starts — fee disputes inevitable
21. **No standard intake script** — every receptionist or paralegal freelances
22. **Pricing flat-fee work below effective hourly rate** — losing money on volume
23. **No dashboard partners see weekly** — strategic decisions made on vibes
24. **Trust interest (IOLTA / mixed trust) not remitted on schedule** — regulatory exposure
25. **Lawyers doing their own intake** — highest-paid people in the firm doing the lowest-leverage work

## GARY VEE × HORMOZI × TATE MINDSET: LAW FIRM EDITION

**Hormozi:** Your realization rate is your pricing IQ. If you're working $100 and billing $78, you're not a lawyer with a billing problem — you're a lawyer who hasn't decided what your hour is worth. Raise the rate, scope the matter, kill the discount.

**Gary Vee:** Law firms in 2026 win on **intake speed, content authority, and reputation density**. The first firm to call back wins the matter 60% of the time. The first firm to answer the question on Google or YouTube becomes the local authority. Your reviews are your moat.

**Tate:** Stop being polite about AR. Clients who don't pay aren't your clients — they're your charity work. Send the 3-tier collection sequence on schedule. Disengage the non-payers. Sleep better. The fear of looking unprofessional is what bleeds firms out.

**The synthesis:** Charge what you're worth. Bill on the day work happens. Collect on the day it's due. Refuse the matter that doesn't pay a retainer. Run intake like a sales operation, run trust like a Swiss bank, run AR like a hospital.
